# NEON CHAOS: Survive the Drunken Arena

**A chaotic neon-soaked 2D arena shooter where survival meets madness.**

Battle endless waves of enemies, collect rewards, and unleash devastating chaos in a world where the rules are constantly changing. Master double jumps, dodge deadly attacks, and survive unpredictable reality-bending events that turn every fight into a unique challenge.

### Features
- **Wave-Based Survival:** Fight increasingly difficult waves of enemies.
- **Unique Enemies:** Face Grunts, Flyers, Tanks, and Snipers, each with distinct behaviors.
- **Chaos Events:** Experience 16 unpredictable events, from gravity flips to meteor showers.
- **Power-Ups & Rewards:** Collect coins, health pickups, and mystery crates.
- **Drunken Physics:** Deal with wobbly movement, drifting aim, and unexpected control disruptions.
- **Dynamic Neon Visuals:** Immerse yourself in a vibrant cyberpunk-inspired arena.
- **Cross-Platform Controls:** Play using keyboard and mouse or mobile touch controls.
- **Survival Challenge:** Push your limits, beat your high score, and see how long you can last.

### How to Play
Move, jump, aim, and shoot to eliminate enemies. Collect rewards, manage your health, and fill the Chaos Meter to trigger unpredictable events. Each wave gets harder, so stay alert and survive as long as possible!

### Controls
- **A / D** or **Arrow Keys** — Move
- **Space / W** — Jump (double jump supported)
- **Mouse** — Aim and shoot
- **J** — Shoot
- **Esc** — Pause

Mobile players can use the on-screen movement, jump, aim, and shoot controls.

### The Goal
Survive the chaos. Defeat the waves. Break your records.

**Can you conquer the arena, or will chaos consume you?**
